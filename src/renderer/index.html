Your devices

Use your Seal Chat ID on more than one computer. Each device has its own address, and contacts can reach you at any of them — it doesn't matter which one you gave them. Your devices pass messages, contacts and chats on to each other through Tor, sealed with your key. Contacts need Seal Chat 1.9 or newer to use your other addresses.

Mailbox

When you're offline, your contacts' messages can wait somewhere other than their own PC — so you get them even if they've gone offline too. Every message is sealed with PGP for you: nobody on the way can read it.

Your mailbox on Cloudflare (free)

A small Cloudflare Worker holds messages for you. Works even when your web host blocks Tor. One-time setup (about 5 minutes — see the help guide, "Mailbox on Cloudflare"):

  1. Copy the Worker script (it's made for this PC) and paste it into a new Worker at dash.cloudflare.com.
  2. Add a D1 database to the Worker with the variable name DB.
  3. Paste the Worker's address here and press Connect.

Files

      
      
      
Files travel over Tor, typically 0.5–3 MB per second: a photo takes seconds, a long video several minutes. Both PCs need Seal Chat running; a big file can arrive over several sessions.

      
Stored files…

      
Sent and received files are kept encrypted in Seal Chat's folder. Removing them frees space; the messages stay ("File removed from this PC").

      

    


    

      
Backup

      
A backup lets you move to a new PC and keep the same Seal Chat ID, contacts and chats. It is encrypted with a password you choose. Files you sent or received are not included (they can be large) — save the ones you want to keep.
